# Drawing, privacy, fill, and OCR

Open **Tools** and search for **Draw & annotate**, **Privacy & emphasis**,
**Color fill**, or **Extract text (OCR)**. On a phone, **More controls** or the
palette action opens all choices; **More photo** restores the large preview.
One finger draws. Two fingers zoom and move the photo without leaving a mark.

## Brushes and annotations

Choose Pen or Highlighter, then a round pen, square tip, marker, airbrush,
calligraphy nib, crayon, or eraser. Pick a swatch or any custom color and adjust
stroke size/opacity. Each stroke keeps the style it had when drawn. The existing
Brush tool also includes a Paint color mode alongside its tonal adjustments.

Drag to place an arrow, line, curve, rectangle, rounded box, ellipse, triangle,
or star. Curves have an adjustable bend. Shapes support fills, separate fill
color/opacity, and dashed outlines; arrows can have heads at both ends.
Highlighter uses multiply blending so dark text stays legible.

Number places a circle at each tap and advances the counter; Next number can be
changed manually. Select / move lets you drag a mark or delete it. Undo and Redo
work per mark before Apply. Clear layer can also be undone. Apply commits an
editable drawing layer; reopen it under Edits to change or erase its marks. The
eraser affects ink in that layer. Source pixels remain available underneath.

## Privacy and emphasis

Choose Blur, Pixelate, Redact, Highlight, or Spotlight, then drag a rectangle,
ellipse, or painted selection. Effect strength controls blur, block size, or
spotlight dimming. Highlight and Redact offer color choices. Multiple spotlights
keep all selected regions clear.

Redact uses an opaque cover in the downloaded JPG/PNG/WebP. Editable projects
and recipes keep reversible edits and the original image. Share the exported
image when you want the covered result. Blur and pixelation are visual effects;
use Redact for opaque coverage.

## Fill

Tap a region with Connected area to flood only adjacent matching pixels. Raise
Color tolerance to include nearby colors. Matching colors fills disconnected
areas of the tapped color, and Entire image covers the whole canvas. Pick a fill
color/opacity or Clear to transparency. PNG and WebP retain transparent areas;
JPG flattens them onto white. Shape interiors can be filled in Draw & annotate.

## Recognize text

OCR reads the visible edited image on your device, including applied or pending
privacy edits. Choose English, simplified/traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean,
Spanish, French, German, Arabic, Hindi, or the English/Chinese/Japanese combined
profile. All models are bundled and available after offline installation finishes.

Use Select region to crop the scan to a line or passage, or scan the whole image.
Recognition shows progress and can be cancelled. Review and edit the result,
show word locations, Copy text, Save TXT, or Add text layer. Copy/TXT keep the
full result. A photo text layer uses up to 300 characters and eight lines and
remains movable/rotatable in the editor.

Printed, well-lit, sharp text works best. Accuracy varies with language and
layout; OCR results should be reviewed. Scans use a maximum 3200 px edge/8 MP
image to bound mobile memory. No photo or recognized text is sent to an OCR
service. [Model sources and checksums](../public/models/ocr/SOURCES.md).
